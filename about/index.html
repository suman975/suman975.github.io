About - Suman Acharya

Architect & Digital Artist

I'm an architect in Pokhara, Nepal, designing thoughtful, functional homes and bringing them to life through 3D visualization. Outside of architecture, I paint digital portraits and sketch the places and people that inspire me.

What I do

Residential Design

Layouts and planning for homes, from concept through construction documentation and municipal approval.

3D Visualization

Realistic exterior and interior renders that show a design clearly before it's built.

Interior Design

Space-efficient interiors with built-in storage, warm materials and integrated lighting.

Commissioned Portraits

Digital paintings of people, families and characters, recorded as a timelapse from sketch to final.
