About - Suman Acharya

Architect & Digital Artist

I am an Architect with a passion for creating thoughtful, functional, and visually compelling spaces. My work spans architectural planning, residential design, interior and exterior visualization, and digital representation, allowing me to bridge creative concepts with practical execution.

I have experience designing residential layouts, developing high-quality 3D visualizations, conducting site inspections, coordinating municipal approval processes, and collaborating throughout the design and construction process. I enjoy transforming ideas into realistic designs that balance aesthetics, functionality, and constructability while meeting client and project requirements.

I hold a Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.) and have worked across all stages of residential projects—from concept development and planning to construction documentation and site coordination. I am proficient in AutoCAD, SketchUp, D5 Render, Lumion, Adobe Photoshop, and other visualization tools, enabling me to communicate design ideas with clarity and precision.

Beyond architecture, I have a strong interest in digital art and visualization. I enjoy exploring new rendering techniques, creating architectural presentations, and continuously improving my design workflow through technology.

I am always eager to connect with architects, designers, engineers, and professionals who share a passion for thoughtful design, innovation, and continuous learning. I welcome opportunities to collaborate on projects that create meaningful, sustainable, and well-designed spaces.
